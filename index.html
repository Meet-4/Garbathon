Q2: InfiniBand Fabric Straggler Isolation across 4,096 GPUs ▼

Scenario: All-Reduce operations intermittently stall during 3D-parallel training across 4,096 GPUs.

  • Metric Telemetry: Correlate NCCL trace events (e.g., `ncclAllReduce` ring times) with hardware counters via DCGM (NVIDIA Data Center GPU Manager).
  • Hardware Inspection: Check for thermal clock throttling, PCIe replay errors, and RoCE PFC (Priority Flow Control) pause frames or dropped packets on specific switches.
  • Health Checks: Execute peer-to-peer bandwidth benchmarks (NCCL tests) in an isolated ring before scheduling batch steps.
Q3: 100TB/Day Streaming Multi-Modal Ingestion & Deduplication ▼

Architecture: Fault-tolerant streaming token ingestion without GPU dataloader starvation.
